Drop your photos in this folder.

Then un-comment the matching lines in the PHOTOS list inside index.html:

  PHOTOS: [
    "photos/1.jpg",
    "photos/2.jpg",
    "photos/3.jpg",
    "photos/4.jpg",
  ],

Tips:
  - 4 to 6 photos is the sweet spot
  - portrait / vertical shots look best (object-position is set to favour faces)
  - keep each file under ~1 MB so the page stays fast on her phone
